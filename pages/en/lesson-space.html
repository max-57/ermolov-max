Independent EdTech product · MVP

Lesson Space

An online lesson room with video calls, a collaborative whiteboard and interactive exercises.

How a lesson works

The teacher sends an invitation. The student opens the room from the link and joins the call. In the same room, they can use the board to explain the material and complete exercises.

My contribution

I designed the flows and interface and built the MVP independently. The web interface uses Vue and Nuxt. I can demonstrate the room and its interactive exercises in a call.
